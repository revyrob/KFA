Chamber Group

For Eligibility for the Provincial Festival

A combination of any recognized orchestral instruments (including harp, guitar, recorder and/or percussion) with or without piano and/or one voice. In the Junior, Intermediate and Senior levels, a group may have a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 9 performers, each having their own part. When a piano is included, the piano part must be original and not an orchestral reduction. There shall be no conductor. Instrumentation and amplification may be used but only as indicated in the original score. A complete score must be provided to the adjudicator and may not be an unauthorized photocopy.

CH1 Junior Chamber Group

Average age 14 years and under, 3–9 performers, time limit 20 minutes. Two contrasting selections, one of which has been performed at the local level.

CH2 Intermediate Chamber Group

Average age 17 years and under, 3–9 performers, time limit 25 minutes. Two contrasting selections, one of which has been performed at the local level.

CH3 Senior Chamber Group

Average age 23 years and under, 3–9 performers, time limit 30 minutes. Two contrasting selections, one of which has been performed at the local level.

It is the responsibility of each participant to read and comply with all the rules governing the Provincial Festival: www.bcprovincials.com

Supplementary Rules

Strings

  1. Please indicate your instrument and years of study on your online entry. To facilitate scheduling, please include the name of your accompanist on the online entry.
  2. All participants will be adjudicated.
  3. All participants will follow Rule 8 of the General Rules, repeated here:
    1. The title and composer of the chosen piece and length of performance time must be stated on the official online entry. No substitutions, additions, or deletions permitted after closing date.
    2. Prior to their class, participants must give a copy of their chosen piece to the Festival Assistant indicating the page with a bookmark upon which has been pencilled the class number and participant's number according to the program. THE USE OF PHOTOCOPIES IS NOT PERMITTED, except to facilitate a page turn or for more than one performer. The performer, adjudicator and accompanist must all use an original score. Please remind all Provincial candidates that they will need ORIGINALS if they go to Provincials.
    3. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will take into consideration its suitability and artistic value.
  4. As an aid to the adjudicator, participants must number the first bar of each line in their score.
  5. No marks will be awarded; instead, outstanding performances will be awarded a Certificate of Merit. In any class, the adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, if there are no outstanding performances in a class, no Certificates will be awarded. Certificates will not be presented publicly but will be given to the student's teachers at the end of the festival session.
  6. Teachers may, at their own discretion, perform with their students in an ensemble of 4 or more. Please indicate this on the online entry and inform the adjudicator's assistant in advance. Such an ensemble would not qualify for an award.
  7. The adjudicator's decisions regarding the awards and provincial nominations will be final. Preference will be given to participants who have entered a minimum of three classes. The adjudicator may choose a Junior A, Junior B, Intermediate, and Senior student to represent our Festival at the Provincial Festival.

Classical Guitar

  1. All participants will be adjudicated.
  2. All participants will follow General Rule 8, repeated here:
    1. The title and composer of the chosen piece and length of performance time must be stated on the official online entry. No substitutions, additions, or deletions are permitted after the closing date.
    2. Prior to their class, participants must give a copy of their chosen piece to the Festival Assistant indicating the page with a bookmark upon which has been pencilled the class number and participant's number. See General Rule 8 regarding photocopies.
    3. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will take into consideration its suitability and artistic value.
  3. No marks will be awarded; instead, outstanding performances will be awarded a Certificate of Merit. In any class the adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, if there are no outstanding performances in a class, no Certificates will be awarded.
  4. Teachers may, at their own discretion, perform with their students in an ensemble. Please indicate this on the online entry and inform the adjudicator's assistant in advance. Such an ensemble would not qualify for an award.
  5. Students wishing to be considered for an award or Provincial Festival nomination must indicate this on the online entry in the space provided.
  6. The adjudicator's decisions regarding the awards and provincial nominations will be final. Preference will be given to participants who have entered a minimum of three classes.

Provincial Eligibility

Strings

Participants interested in qualifying for a provincial nomination must have performed in a minimum of 2 solo classes in the Strings division and have received a mark of 85 or better in at least 2 of those classes. If nominated to participate in the provincial festival, the nominees may also enter the Optional Parallel Canadian Class at their respective level.

The strings syllabus of the Kootenay Festival of the Arts is not necessarily the same as the provincial syllabus; for further information see the Performing Arts BC website.
