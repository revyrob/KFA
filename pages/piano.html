Supplementary Rules

Because the festival should be a learning experience, emphasizing participation and enjoyment of music, the following format will be used for the Piano Section:

  1. No marks will be awarded. Instead, each outstanding performance will be awarded a Certificate of Merit. In any class, the adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, no certificates will be granted if there are no outstanding performances in a class. Memory is recommended but not compulsory. Certificates will be given only for memorized performances. Each student will receive a written adjudication.
  2. Repeats are optional but may be limited or disallowed by the adjudicator. Da capos should be observed.
  3. All participants will follow Rule 8 of the General Rules, repeated here:
    1. The title and composer of the chosen piece and length of performance time will be stated on the official online entry. No substitutions, additions, or deletions are permitted after the closing date.
    2. Participants will deliver to the festival assistant a copy of their music indicated with a bookmark upon which has been pencilled at the top the class number and participant's number according to the program. This music will be delivered before the class performance. The sharing of books or use of photocopies is strictly limited. Accompanist will use an original score. See General Rule 7.
    3. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will consider its suitability and artistic value.
  4. As an aid to the adjudicator, participants will number the first bar of each line in their score.
  5. A Highlights Concert with the Awards presentation will be held on Saturday, April 10th at 2:00 p.m. at The Bailey Theatre. Participants who have received Certificates of Merit may be asked to perform at this concert.
  6. The adjudicator solely decides which performers receive awards and recommends performers for the Highlights Concert. The piano coordinator will notify the teachers, and it will be the teachers' responsibility to notify the selected students.
  7. The adjudicator may choose students to represent our festival at the provincial level in Junior, Intermediate, Senior, and Provincial Excellence classes. Preference will be given to participants who have received three Certificates of Merit.

Provincial Eligibility

Participants interested in qualifying for a provincial nomination will have performed in a minimum of two solo classes in the Piano division and have received a mark of 85 or better in at least two of those classes. If nominated to participate in the provincial festival, the nominees may also enter the Optional Parallel Canadian Class at their respective level.

The piano syllabus of the Kootenay Festival of the Arts is not necessarily the same as the provincial syllabus. For further information regarding provincials, go to the Performing Arts BC website at bcprovincials.com.
