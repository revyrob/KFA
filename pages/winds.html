Instruments & Bands

Woodwinds: classes may include Flute, Clarinet, Oboe, Bassoon, English Horn, Contra-Bassoon and Saxophone. Students are permitted to use two instruments of the same family, e.g. Piccolo and Flute, or A and B flat clarinet, in the same competition. Please indicate your instrument on your online entry.

Brass: kindly use the following categories, stating on the online entry which instrument(s) will be played. Brass classes will include the standard orchestral and band brass instruments. Students are permitted to use two instruments of the same family.

Bands: school and community bands are highly encouraged to participate. On the online entry, please include the school/community, the size of the band, and what type of band (concert, jazz, etc.) or ensemble. The category will be BANDS and ENSEMBLES.

Chamber Group

For Eligibility for the Provincial Festival

A combination of any recognized orchestral instruments (including harp, guitar, recorder and/or percussion) with or without piano and/or one voice. In the Junior, Intermediate and Senior levels, a group may have a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 9 performers, each having their own part. When a piano is included, the piano part must be original and not an orchestral reduction. There shall be no conductor. Instrumentation and amplification may be used but only as indicated in the original score. A complete score must be provided to the adjudicator and may not be an unauthorized photocopy.

CH1 Junior Chamber Group

Average age 14 years and under, 3–9 performers, time limit 20 minutes. Two contrasting selections, one of which has been performed at the local level.

CH2 Intermediate Chamber Group

Average age 17 years and under, 3–9 performers, time limit 25 minutes. Two contrasting selections, one of which has been performed at the local level.

CH3 Senior Chamber Group

Average age 23 years and under, 3–9 performers, time limit 30 minutes. Two contrasting selections, one of which has been performed at the local level.

It is the responsibility of each participant to read and comply with all the rules governing the Provincial Festival: www.bcprovincials.com

Supplementary Rules

  1. All participants will be adjudicated.
  2. All participants will follow General Rule 8, repeated here:
    1. The title and composer of the chosen piece and length of performance time must be stated on the official online entry. No substitutions, additions, or deletions are permitted after the closing date.
    2. Before their class, participants must give a copy of their chosen piece to the Festival Assistant indicating the page with a bookmark upon which the class number and participant's number have been pencilled. See General Rule 8 regarding photocopies.
    3. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will take into consideration its suitability and artistic value.
  3. No marks will be awarded; instead, outstanding performances will be awarded a Certificate of Merit. In any class, the adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, if there are no exceptional performances in a class, no Certificates will be awarded.
  4. Teachers may, at their discretion, perform with their students in an ensemble. Please indicate this on the online entry and inform the adjudicator's assistant in advance. Such an ensemble would not qualify for an award.
  5. Students wishing to be considered for an award or Provincial Festival nomination must indicate this on the online entry in the space provided.
  6. The adjudicator's decisions regarding the awards and provincial nominations will be final. Preference will be given to participants who have entered a minimum of three classes.

Provincial Eligibility

Participants interested in qualifying for a provincial nomination must have performed in a minimum of two solo classes in the Woodwind division and have obtained a mark of 85 or better in at least two of those classes. See the Performing Arts BC website for the provincial syllabus.
