We Need Your Help

Kootenay Festival of the Arts is sponsored by the Kootenay Music Festival Society, Trail, BC, and is proud to be affiliated with Performing Arts BC.

The Kootenay Music Festival Society is dedicated to providing an opportunity for young performers to achieve excellence. To encourage participation, we would like to keep entrance fees low as well as obtain high-quality adjudicators. We would like to continue to reward excellence with a variety of awards. As we depend entirely on voluntary assistance and support, we are asking you to consider a cash donation to the festival.

Cheques payable to Kootenay Music Festival Society, Box 1724, 1889 Nevada St., Rossland, BC V0G 1Y0. E-transfers payable to treasurertrail@kootenayfestivalofthearts.ca (please add specific information to the e-transfer message). Donors will be identified to be thanked on the Kootenay Festival of the Arts website unless they wish to remain anonymous, and donations can be applied to an award for a category of your choice.

Supplementary Rules

Each genre has its own supplementary rules, which apply in addition to the General Rules. Open a genre below to read them.

Dance
  1. Time limits (entries are not to exceed):
    Solos3 minutes
    Song and Dance Solos4 minutes
    Duos5 minutes
    Trios5 minutes
    Groups7 minutes
    Stage Production Large Groups10 minutes
  2. Age categories (age as of December 31st before the upcoming Festival):
    • Duo & Trio categories: enter the class of the oldest member.
    • Group category: age is determined by adding the ages of all the dancers and dividing by the number of dancers; totals ending in ".5" and higher are rounded up to the next age.
  3. Music requirements: all music must be uploaded through the official registration platform at the time of registration. Music submissions received through any other method will not be accepted. Studio representatives are responsible for ensuring that all music files are uploaded correctly, clearly identified, and matched to the appropriate entry before the registration deadline. As a precaution against technical issues, studio representatives are encouraged to bring backup copies of all music files to the festival.
  4. Limited sets and props are suggested where allowed. All props must be an integral part of the performance and be handled by the participant without assistance from the festival staff.
  5. All performances must be in age-appropriate costumes.
  6. Lip-synching is only permitted in Theatrical Dance and Variety.
  7. Live singing is only permitted in Song and Dance.
  8. Lyrics are not permitted in any Ballet categories except for Contemporary Ballet. Vocalization (humming, whistling, chanting, etc.) is acceptable.
  9. In all Modern categories, lyrics, music with vocalization, vocalization without music, and the spoken word are permitted. It is recommended that every effort be made to select a musical piece that is stylistically appropriate to the Modern discipline.
  10. Any reference to violence or derogatory sexual content or language will not be permitted and use of such will result in disqualification.
  11. A competitor may not use the same dance in a subsequent year.
  12. Duos, trios, and groups will be submitted on one online entry with all names and festival ages listed.
  13. The adjudicator's decisions regarding awards and provincial nominees will be final. The adjudicator may choose to select Junior, Intermediate, and Senior participants to represent our Festival at the provincial level, provided all requirements have been met for both Performing Arts BC and the Kootenay Festival of the Arts. Please be advised that an adjudicator can also choose NOT to nominate a dancer even though they have met the stated requirements if they feel the dancer is not ready to compete at the Provincial level. The adjudicator may also select Merited Participants from the same groups to attend the Provincial Festival as a Merited Participant, not a competitor.
  14. The performer is responsible for ensuring that copies of recorded music comply with all copyright restrictions that apply to the source material.

Pianoforte

Because the festival should be a learning experience, emphasizing participation and enjoyment of music, the following format will be used for the Piano Section:

  1. No marks will be awarded. Instead, each outstanding performance will be awarded a Certificate of Merit. In any class, the adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, no certificates will be granted if there are no outstanding performances in a class. Memory is recommended but not compulsory. Certificates will be given only for memorized performances. Each student will receive a written adjudication.
  2. Repeats are optional but may be limited or disallowed by the adjudicator. Da capos should be observed.
  3. All participants will follow Rule 8 of the General Rules, repeated here:
    1. The title and composer of the chosen piece and length of performance time will be stated on the official online entry. No substitutions, additions, or deletions are permitted after the closing date.
    2. Participants will deliver to the festival assistant a copy of their music indicated with a bookmark upon which has been pencilled at the top the class number and participant's number according to the program. This music will be delivered before the class performance. The sharing of books or use of photocopies is strictly limited. Accompanist will use an original score. See General Rule 7.
    3. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will consider its suitability and artistic value.
  4. As an aid to the adjudicator, participants will number the first bar of each line in their score.
  5. A Highlights Concert with the Awards presentation will be held on Saturday, April 10th at 2:00 p.m. at The Bailey Theatre. Participants who have received Certificates of Merit may be asked to perform at this concert.
  6. The adjudicator solely decides which performers receive awards and recommends performers for the Highlights Concert. The piano coordinator will notify the teachers, and it will be the teachers' responsibility to notify the selected students.
  7. The adjudicator may choose students to represent our festival at the provincial level in Junior, Intermediate, Senior, and Provincial Excellence classes. Preference will be given to participants who have received three Certificates of Merit.

Voice & Speech Arts

Speech & Dramatic Arts

  1. Choice of one poem per class.
  2. When "Own Choice", Rule 8 of the General Rules applies. Specifically for Speech Arts:
    1. The title and Author of the chosen piece and length of performance time must be stated on the official online entry. No substitutions, additions, or deletions are permitted after the closing date.
    2. Participants will deliver to the festival assistant a copy of own choice piece indicated with a bookmark upon which has been pencilled at the top: the class number and participant number according to the program. This piece must be delivered prior to class performance. The use of photocopies is strictly limited. See General Rule No. 7. Note: bible selections are in the public domain. Please provide, for the adjudicator, a photocopy of any bible selections used.
    3. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will consider suitability and artistic value.
  3. The adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, if there are no outstanding performances in a class, no Certificates may be awarded.
  4. The adjudicator's decisions regarding awards and provincial nominees will be final. The adjudicator may choose a Junior, Intermediate, and Senior student to represent our Festival at the provincial level. Preference will be given to those participants who have performed in a minimum of three classes, including selections of Prose, Drama, and Poetry.

Choral & Vocal Arts

  1. All participants will follow Rule 8 of the General Rules (repeated here):
    1. The title and composer of the chosen piece and length of performance time must be stated on the official online entry.
    2. Participants will deliver to the festival assistant a copy of their piece indicated with a bookmark upon which has been pencilled at the top the class number and participant's number according to the program before class performance. The use of photocopies is strictly limited. See copyright rule No. 7.
    3. Please mark bar numbers on all music viewed by the adjudicator.
    4. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will take into consideration its suitability and artistic value.
      *Accompanist will use original music, not photocopies, and original music will be provided for the adjudicator.
  2. Each outstanding performance will be awarded a Certificate of Merit. In any class the adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, if there are no outstanding performances in a class, no certificates will be awarded.
  3. Conductors are not allowed to sing with their choirs or classes.
  4. The adjudicator's decisions regarding the awards and provincial competitors will be final. The adjudicator may choose a junior, intermediate, or senior student to represent our Festival at the Provincial Festival. Preference will be given to participants receiving three Certificates of Merit.
  5. At the local festival, memorization is recommended, but not compulsory. Memorization at the Provincial Festival is mandatory in all Voice Classes.

Woodwinds, Brass & Bands
  1. All participants will be adjudicated.
  2. All participants will follow General Rule 8, repeated here:
    1. The title and composer of the chosen piece and length of performance time must be stated on the official online entry. No substitutions, additions, or deletions are permitted after the closing date.
    2. Before their class, participants must give a copy of their chosen piece to the Festival Assistant indicating the page with a bookmark upon which the class number and participant's number have been pencilled. See General Rule 8 regarding photocopies.
    3. In "Own Choice" selection, the adjudicator will take into consideration its suitability and artistic value.
  3. No marks will be awarded; instead, outstanding performances will be awarded a Certificate of Merit. In any class, the adjudicator may award several Certificates of Merit; however, if there are no exceptional performances in a class, no Certificates will be awarded.
  4. Teachers may, at their discretion, perform with their students in an ensemble. Please indicate this on the online entry and inform the adjudicator's assistant in advance. Such an ensemble would not qualify for an award.
  5. Students wishing to be considered for an award or Provincial Festival nomination must indicate this on the online entry in the space provided.
  6. The adjudicator's decisions regarding the awards and provincial nominations will be final. Preference will be given to participants who have entered a minimum of three classes.
